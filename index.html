Vol. 1How to benchmark RSI

September 2026 · draft for review

Recursive self-improvement is now something frontier labs try to measure, but what it is, and how to benchmark it, remain open questions. Whatever the definition, training an AI model end to end on real infrastructure sits at its core. This volume audits ten coding-agent benchmark suites against the life cycle of production LLMs and maps what their environments actually exercise.
